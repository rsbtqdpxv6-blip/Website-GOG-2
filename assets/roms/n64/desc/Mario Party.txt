Mario has battled Bowser, saved the Mushroom Kingdom, and raced through countless adventures, but now it's time to party! In Mario Party, Nintendo brings the most exciting bash to the N64, where Mario and friends compete in a board game-style showdown packed with mini-games.
Players roll dice to move across themed boards, each filled with surprises and over fifty mini-games. Competitions range from free-for-all challenges to two-on-two and three-against-one battles, testing skills in racing, puzzles, and quick reflexes. Winning games earns coins and stars, determining the ultimate party champion.
Game boards feature animated elements, hazards, and special challenges. Mario, Luigi, Peach, Yoshi, Wario, Donkey Kong, and Bowser all join the fun, each with their own board. While Mario Party offers solo play, the real fun comes with friends, ensuring an unforgettable multiplayer experience full of laughter, strategy, and late-night excitement.
