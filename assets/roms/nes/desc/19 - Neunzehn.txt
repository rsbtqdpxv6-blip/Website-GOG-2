The game roughly consists of a strategy screen where you move your subordinate troops across the island that is the stage, and a combat mode where you handle battles when troops come into contact with each other on the strategy screen.

The element of searching for the "almighty stone" in the story does not actually exist in the game. It is a game consisting only of pure tactical and strategic elements, with the goal of ultimately defeating all three factions other than one's own.

This work has a very unique operation system, and the movement method is radio-controlled, similar to ``Bangering Bay'' (1984).
