Bionic Commando puts you in the role of a specially chosen soldier equipped with a unique bionic arm. This arm extends and retracts, granting you unprecedented strength and agility. Armed with rapid-fire, armor-piercing, and heat-seeking weapons, you are your people's last hope after two years of relentless invasion. Your mission takes you across a heavily patrolled desert and into the enemy stronghold itself. What seems like an impossible challenge becomes a battle only the Bionic Commando can endure and win.
