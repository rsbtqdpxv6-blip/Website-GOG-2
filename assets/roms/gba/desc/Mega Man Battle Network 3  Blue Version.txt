Join MegaMan.EXE, his operator Lan, and friends as they keep the net safe from viruses and cyber menaces. You'll travel through new virtual and real worlds, fight new bosses, interact with new characters, and even find new Battle Chips. It's cyber mayhem at its best! Mega Man Battle Network 3 has two unique versions: Blue and White. Each version contains unique Battle Chips, characters, and styles for MegaMan.EXE to use.
