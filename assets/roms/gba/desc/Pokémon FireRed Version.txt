In this adventure, you travel through the Kanto region, form a close bond with your Pokémon, and catch various species to complete the Pokédex. Challenge other trainers, stand up to Team Rocket, and battle the Gym Leaders to earn all eight badges. With these in your possession, you can take on the Elite Four and ultimately face the reigning Champion in a final, decisive battle. Your goal: Become the best trainer and catch them all!
