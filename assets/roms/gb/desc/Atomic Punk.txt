Dynablaster lets you battle through classic Bomberman action in single-player or take on new twists in Game A, where you explore zones from a world map in any order. The faster you clear a level and the more blocks you destroy, the more gold you earn to spend on power-ups like extra lives or extended blast ranges. Before entering each new area, you choose which of these panels to bring along. In multiplayer mode, you face off in classic Bomberman fashion – either in Panel Mode, where players start weak and gather upgrades, or in Powerful Mode, where you launch into the chaos with full firepower. Strategy, timing, and nerves of steel will decide whether you dominate or go out in a glorious blast.
