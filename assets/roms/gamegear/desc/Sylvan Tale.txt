In "Sylvan Tale," you play as a young warrior who is mysteriously teleported to the land of Verda by a glowing object. A voice tasks you with collecting six magical droplets from fortresses to save the inhabitants of Verda. You explore towns, traverse dungeons, engage in real-time combat against enemies, and collect items that allow you to transform into various creatures with unique abilities. As a mole, you can dig through rocks; as a merman, you can swim; as a turtle, you use your shell for defense; as a mouse, you squeeze through small holes; and as a bird, you can fly and evade attacks.
