In the first of three Super NES games, choose from four cars with distinct top speed, fuel consumption, boost power, and tire grip. Race across 32 challenging tracks set in countries including Brazil, the USA, France, and Japan, either solo or in two-player split-screen mode.

Longer courses feature pit stops for refueling, while iron plates and rocks create extra hazards. In single-player mode, the computer opponent has a slight speed advantage. A password system lets you continue later, but you must finish at least 5th to advance.
