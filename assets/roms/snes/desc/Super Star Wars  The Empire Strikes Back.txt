Super Star Wars: The Empire Strikes Back takes you on an action-packed journey through iconic moments – from the icy battles on Hoth to the mysterious training with Yoda and the dramatic confrontation with Darth Vader. Many scenes are expanded or reimagined, adding new locations and enemies while introducing surprising twists. You switch between Luke, Han and Chewbacca, each playing differently with unique special abilities – from destructive spins to Luke's lightsaber defense and unlockable Force powers. Alongside classic platforming sections, vehicle stages in the snowspeeder, Millennium Falcon and X-Wing deliver cinematic variety, while hidden collectibles, upgrades and new powers constantly encourage you to explore every stage thoroughly.
